Our Philosophy

East Meets
East.

Tenka was born from a radical idea: that Japanese and Chinese cuisines are not opposites, but reflections. Japan's precision and restraint. China's depth and generosity. Together, they form a complete culinary universe.

We honor both traditions by understanding their souls. Our chefs have trained in Kyoto and Chengdu, Tokyo and Hong Kong. Every plate is a conversation across centuries.

The Art

Crafted Across
Two Worlds

Every technique, every ingredient, every detail is chosen from the finest of both traditions. We build bridges, not walls, between two of the world's greatest cuisines.

01

Knife & Wok

Japanese precision cutting meets the explosive energy of wok hei. Two philosophies of fire and blade, united in our open kitchen.

02

Fermentation

Miso and koji from Japan. Douchi and fermented bean curd from China. Our cellar bridges two ancient traditions of umami.

03

Binchotan & Wok

Japanese white charcoal at 1,000°C meets the seasoned wok at 500°C. Two heats, two souls, one kitchen.

04

Tea & Spirit

Matcha ceremony meets Chinese gongfu tea service. Rare Japanese sake alongside aged Chinese baijiu and Huangjiu.

05

Seasonal Soul

Japan's shun and China's shi-ling — both celebrate the fleeting beauty of each season. Our menu changes daily with nature's rhythm.

06

Harmony

The final plate balances Japanese minimalism with Chinese abundance. Every dish is a meditation on harmony between two worlds.
